**h,** Reconstruction of the paired Return-minus-Naive half-coverage efficiency difference. Each Return session was simulated using either its own policies or the policies measured in the same animal's Naive session. The common four-policy and depth-support cohort contained 54 Return sessions and 17 matched Naive baselines from 17 animals. The four-policy walk reconstructed 40.5% [28.6, 54.1] of the observed paired difference; the depth-varying model reconstructed 86.7% [68.5, 107.2]. Intervals are 95% animal-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. Reconstruction is contemporaneous and is neither prospective prediction nor variance explained.

Filled group-color circles show the session-weighted coordinate means of the observed and simulated efficiencies; hollow circles retain individual sessions.
